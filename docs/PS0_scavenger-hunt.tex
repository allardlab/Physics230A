\maketitle


Find values for the following biological quantities.
You may use Google, Wikipedia, textbooks, peer-reviewed papers, large language models, or anything else, but you must cite your sources\footnote{and those sources must have provenance (i.e., they can cite their sources)}.
One valuable source is: \texttt{bionumbers.org}.
In cases where answers need to be qualified, do so.
Are there ranges? Are there a few exceptions or many exceptions?
Are these order-of-magnitude estimates?

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  The number of amino acids in the average human protein
\end{enumerate}

The size of (in meters or other SI unit):

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\setcounter{enumi}{1}
\tightlist
\item
  Longest nerves in humans
\item
  A eukaryotic cell
\item
  A bacterial cell
\item
  An organelle, like the eukaryotic nucleus
\item
  The width of the plasma membrane
\item
  Your favorite protein
\item
  The resolution of a traditional light microscope
\item
  The resolution of a traditional electron microscope
\item
  A water molecule
\item
  The width of DNA
\end{enumerate}

The time for (in seconds or other SI unit):

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\setcounter{enumi}{11}
\tightlist
\item
  Turnover of cells in the human body
\item
  The generation time (time between divisions) of a bacterium in lab conditions
\item
  Transcription of one DNA basepair to RNA
\item
  Translation of one amino acid in an RNA into protein
\item
  Human reflex
\item
  Protein folding
\end{enumerate}

The energy of:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\setcounter{enumi}{17}
\tightlist
\item
  The reaction ATP \(\rightarrow\) ADP-Pi
\item
  The thermal energy unit \(k_B T\) (where \(k_B\) is Boltzmann's constant and \(T\) is the temperature from absolute zero)
\item
  Moving one electron against the electric field across the membrane of a cell
\end{enumerate}
