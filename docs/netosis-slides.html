Neutrophil biology

  • Neutrophils are part of the innate immune system
  • They constantly patrol for invaders, live for between 24 hours and 5 days
  • Kill by degranulation, phagocytosis, NETosis

NETosis

  • Neutrophil Extracellular Traps (NETs) are expelled when neutrophils are activated by danger signals (bacteria, hypoxia, cholesterol crystals, etc.)
  • Can kill bacteria, fungi, viruses

Urban CF, …, Zychlinsky A; Cell Microbiol 2006

What causes nuclear rupture?

During NETosis, there are changes which may modulate nuclear pressure:

  • PAD4 mediated histone citrullination and neutrophil elastase mediated histone cleavage
    • Increase macromolecule number
  • DNA decompaction
    • Increases entropic elasticity
  • PAD4 mediated histone citrullination changes charge balance between histones and DNA

Chirivi et al., J Clin Cell Immunol 2013

Molecular requirements for NETosis

  • Calcium influx
  • Pad4 is required for NETosis
  • Pad4 citrullinates histones
  • Pad4 decondenses DNA
  • Neutrophil elastase is required for NETosis
  • Neutrophil elastase cleaves histones
  • Commonality: changes to DNA condensation

Selina K Jorch; Paul Kubes Nature Medicine 2017
