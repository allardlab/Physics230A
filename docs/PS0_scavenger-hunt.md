# Problem Set 0: Scavenger hunt
Jun Allard

Find values for the following biological quantities. You may use Google,
Wikipedia, textbooks, peer-reviewed papers, large language models, or
anything else, but you must cite your sources[^1]. One valuable source
is: `bionumbers.org`. In cases where answers need to be qualified, do
so. Are there ranges? Are there a few exceptions or many exceptions? Are
these order-of-magnitude estimates?

1.  The number of amino acids in the average human protein

The size of (in meters or other SI unit):

2.  Longest nerves in humans
3.  A eukaryotic cell
4.  A bacterial cell
5.  An organelle, like the eukaryotic nucleus
6.  The width of the plasma membrane
7.  Your favorite protein
8.  The resolution of a traditional light microscope
9.  The resolution of a traditional electron microscope
10. A water molecule
11. The width of DNA

The time for (in seconds or other SI unit):

12. Turnover of cells in the human body
13. The generation time (time between divisions) of a bacterium in lab
    conditions
14. Transcription of one DNA basepair to RNA
15. Translation of one amino acid in an RNA into protein
16. Human reflex
17. Protein folding

The energy of:

18. The reaction ATP $\rightarrow$ ADP-Pi
19. The thermal energy unit $k_B T$ (where $k_B$ is Boltzmann’s constant
    and $T$ is the temperature from absolute zero)
20. Moving one electron against the electric field across the membrane
    of a cell

[^1]: and those sources must have provenance (i.e., they can cite their
    sources)
